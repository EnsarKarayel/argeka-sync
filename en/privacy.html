Privacy

ARGEKA Sync is self-hosted. Database credentials, transfer jobs and run logs are stored in the user's own environment.

This public website provides product information, download links and support documentation. It does not ask visitors for ERP credentials or payment information.

The site may include Google AdSense account verification metadata and ads.txt. If ads are enabled later, Google and partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery.

Public site and local app

The public website provides product information, downloads and support documentation. The installed ARGEKA Sync application runs on the user's own machine or server. ERP credentials, customer records and transfer logs are not collected through this public website.

Local application data remains in the user's own environment, so privacy also depends on local access control, backup and database permission policies.

Advertising and measurement transparency

The website may contain Google AdSense account verification metadata and an ads.txt file to prove publisher ownership. During review mode, ad display scripts can be intentionally limited while the public product documentation is improved. If ads are enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads.

The public website is separate from the self-hosted application. Installing the product does not require visitors to upload ERP data to this website.
